# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 6, 2026_

## 1. Scope and contact

This policy covers the **Goodfellows Republic website and Goodfellows Republic Discord bot**, together referred to as our services. Goodfellows Republic operates these services for its community and internal administration.

For privacy questions or requests about either service, **open a ticket through the Republic's Discord ticket system** and identify it as a privacy request. No email contact is currently designated by this policy. See [Contact](/contact) for guidance.

## 2. Website information

The website provides public information and the [Constitution](/constitution). It does not include analytics trackers or information-collection forms. Reading the website does not itself create a citizenship record with the bot.

Your theme preference is stored locally in your browser so the website can remember light or dark mode. Website search, where available, runs locally in your browser.

GitHub Pages hosts the site. The organization emblem loads from GitHub, and fonts load from Google Fonts. These providers receive network requests, which can include your IP address, browser information, and requested resource, and handle them under their own privacy policies.

## 3. Discord bot information

The bot stores the following records:

| Record type | Information and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Moderation logs | User IDs, usernames, and moderation records, used to administer community rules, review incidents, and handle appeals. |
| Citizenship records | Information recorded through the citizenship process, used to administer citizenship and related community participation. |
| Votes | Voting records, used to administer and maintain records of community votes. Do not assume a vote is anonymous unless the particular voting process explicitly states that it is. |
| Tickets | Ticket content submitted through the ticket system, used to respond to requests, resolve issues, and retain a record of those interactions. |

The bot receives information through Discord when people interact with its features or when moderation and administrative actions are recorded. Ticket content can include messages and other information that you or others submit. Do not include passwords, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information in tickets.

## 4. Retention

**Moderation logs, citizenship records, votes, and tickets are stored indefinitely. There is no automatic deletion schedule for these records.** Closing a ticket, leaving the server, or ceasing to use the bot does not automatically delete an existing record.

This is the routine retention policy, not a waiver of rights under applicable law. Requests for access, correction, deletion, or limits on processing can be made through the ticket system. A request may require review of legal obligations, other people's information, and legitimate recordkeeping needs; deletion is not guaranteed in every case. Where applicable law requires deletion or other action, that requirement takes precedence over routine indefinite retention.

## 5. Access, handling, and third parties

Bot records are used for the community and administrative purposes described above. Moderation, citizenship, voting, and ticket functions involve the responsible administrators or officials. Do not treat the ticket system as an end-to-end encrypted or confidential legal-advice channel.

Discord processes information exchanged through its platform under its own [Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy). Website hosting, font, and image providers also process requests as described above. External websites and services have their own policies. Records may also need to be disclosed when required by applicable law.

## 6. Your requests

Open a **privacy ticket** in the Republic's Discord ticket system to request:

- Access to information concerning you.
- Correction of inaccurate information.
- Deletion of information or a review of its continued retention.
- Information about how your records are handled.

Describe the record type and request, and provide your Discord user ID where relevant. Administrators may need to verify your identity before acting. Do not post personal records or identity documents in public channels. If you cannot access the ticket system, use the public organization profile linked on the [contact page](/contact) to ask how to reach an administrator, without publishing private information.

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have additional privacy rights, including the right to complain to a relevant data protection authority. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## 7. Updates

Changes will be published on this page with an updated date. This single policy applies to both the website and the bot. Related conditions are in our [Terms of Service](/terms).
