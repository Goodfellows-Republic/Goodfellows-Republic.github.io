# Contact

[Join the Goodfellows Republic Discord server →](https://discord.gg/9TQ76stCZZ)

## Questions and support

For questions about Goodfellows Republic, the website, or the Discord bot, **open a ticket through the Republic's Discord ticket system**. Use the ticket entry point available in the Republic's Discord community.

## Privacy requests

For access, correction, deletion, or other questions about your information, open a ticket and identify it as a **privacy request**. Include your Discord user ID where relevant and a description of what you need. Do not post private records or sensitive personal information in public channels.

The shared [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and [Terms of Service](/terms) apply to both the website and Discord bot. No email contact is currently designated.

## Cannot access tickets?

Use the organization's public profile to ask how to reach an administrator. Do not publish personal records, ticket contents, or sensitive information there.
